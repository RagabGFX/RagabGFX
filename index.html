04 / About

Design with a

I'm Mohamed Ragab — a graphic designer based in Egypt, working across Saudi Arabia and the Gulf for clubs, leagues and tournaments. I work at Sport360 agency, where matchdays move fast and every post has to land.

RagabGFX is my practice for sports, identities, campaigns and high-impact digital visuals. Strong concepts, clean systems, and details that make a visual feel finished rather than simply decorated.
